A saddle designed primarily for MTB and gravel, suitable for a wide range of riders thanks to a very versatile shell. Its shape is based on a shell similar to the road Shortfit, but it has several off-road features: the nose is extra padded for steep climbs and the rear is cut away so your shorts don't catch when riding behind the saddle.

Who it suits

  • MTB and gravel
  • Steep climbs on the nose of the saddle
  • Riding behind the saddle on descents
