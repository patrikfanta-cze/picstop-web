When to service your suspension

Every manufacturer recommends a service at least once a year. Regular maintenance keeps your fork performing the way the manufacturer intended.

After every ride

Wipe off the dirt and treat the wiper seals with silicone oil.

50 h

Lower leg service, oil change, wiper seal check and replacement.

100–200 h / 125 h

Full seasonal service of the air and damper chambers (RockShox / FOX).

400–600 h

Dropper post service.

A service shop is not a car wash for disasters

  • Washing your bike is your jobDirt eats your chain and cassette. Ongoing care is your responsibility.
  • Before every rideCheck your brakes, wheel mounting and any play.
  • During the seasonMeasure your chain. Replacing it in time saves a lot of money.
