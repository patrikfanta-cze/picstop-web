The Tyro 2.0 carbon wheels have a completely newly developed, wider rim that meets the needs of modern tyres. The hubs are made by FFWD with the well-known DT Swiss star-ratchet system and industrial bearings. They are laced with straight-pull Sapim Sprint spokes and brass nipples for the required stiffness and durability. The wheels are very sturdy and suitable for road and light gravel.

Who it suits

  • Road and light gravel
  • Riders considering an upgrade to carbon wheels
  • Anyone who wants sturdy wheels for everyday riding
